Privacy

Your audio stays on your computer.

MasterScope analyses files inside your web browser. Nothing you drop is uploaded to any server.

What is stored

  • Version history: the numbers from each file you analyse (never the audio) are saved in your browser's local storage so you can compare versions. Clear them any time from Compare, Version history, or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Your theme choice (dark or light), in local storage.

Visit counts

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. We see totals only (pages visited, countries, browsers), never who you are.

Sponsors

Sponsor links are ordinary links. Sponsors don't receive any information about you, and no advertising trackers are loaded.

The Liminal plug-in

Liminal collects nothing and has no ads. It only saves the files you ask it to save (presets, reports and the release pack) and remembers its window size.
