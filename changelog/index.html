Liminal 1.0

Download coming soon

The first public release.

  • Codec-safe ceiling: keeps the peaks listeners get after Opus (YouTube) and Ogg Vorbis (Spotify) under your ceiling, measured on your song with the real encoders. All codecs adds a margin for MP3 and AAC.
  • Learn by loudness or density: aim for −14 LUFS, or for a PLR such as 10, and Learn sets the threshold in one listen.
  • Listen through Opus: hear the output through a real Opus encoder in real time.
  • Optimise: tries about 30 setups at your target, all equally loud, and ranks them by punch.
  • Punch Guard: a limiter into a clipper, so the drums keep their snap at the same loudness.
  • Mix Doctor: shows which part of the mix makes the peaks the limiter fights, and what to change.
  • Reference track at matched loudness, and a release pack that writes Streaming, Club, Apple Music and YouTube masters from one listen.
  • A rebuilt spectrum, a 2D and 3D spectrogram, a punch meter, a stretchable window and full screen.
  • 14 factory presets, Import preset (for MasterScope's suggestions), A/B, undo and session reports.

Details for each feature are in the user guide.
