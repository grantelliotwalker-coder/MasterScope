Features

Everything Liminal does, from setting the level by hand to the helpers under Tools.

Simple by hand

Choose a style, pull the threshold down until it sounds right, and the ceiling keeps the peaks safe. The settings you set once and leave live under Advanced; the helpers live under Tools.

Learn, in one listen

A second opinion: pick a loudness or a density (PLR), press Learn and play the song. Liminal renders what it heard through hidden copies of itself and suggests the threshold that lands on the target. Undo puts it back.

Safe after encoding

The Streaming ceiling keeps the peaks a listener gets after Opus and Vorbis under your ceiling, measured on your song with the real encoders. All codecs adds a margin for MP3 and AAC.

Optimise

Tries about 30 setups at your target, all equally loud, and ranks them by punch. Apply the best and Liminal re-learns it on the whole song.

Punch Guard

A limiter into a clipper: the tips of the drum hits go to the clipper, so the mix isn't turned down around every hit. More snap at the same loudness.

Mix Doctor

Shows which part of the mix makes the peaks the limiter fights, how much a little peak control there would save, and what to do about it.

See everything

A rebuilt 8192-point spectrum (flat for pink noise, average, peak hold, input and reference overlays, note readout) and a 2D and 3D spectrogram with a Delta view of what limiting removes.

Hear what matters

Level match plays your mix at the output's loudness. Hear what's removed plays only what the limiter takes. Codec check plays the output through a real Opus encoder. Reference track plays a song you like at your loudness.

Release pack

Streaming, Club, Apple Music and YouTube masters written straight to disk from one listen, each a 24-bit WAV with its MasterScope report.

Live meters

Loudness (momentary, short-term, integrated), true peak, the peak a listener gets after encoding, PLR, punch, gain reduction, correlation and stereo width, all as you play.

Snapshot

One click copies every setting and the current meter readings as text, ready to paste into a message or a forum post when you want to show exactly how Liminal is set.

Any size, any look

Stretch the window to any shape (extra room goes to the graphs) or press full screen, and pick Dark, Light or a hardware look: Console, Rack or Vintage.

Listen

The final section of Jai, one of Grant Walker's own tracks, from four bars before it to the end of the song: the mixdown as it left the mix, then the same mix through Liminal. Switch between them as it plays. Level match is on, so the master plays at the mixdown's loudness and you hear the difference in punch and tone, not just volume. Headphones or good speakers help.

Themes

Pick a look from the theme button at the top right. The hardware skins dress Liminal like studio gear, with metal plates, knurled knobs and lit buttons, while the meters and graphs stay on the same clear screens in every theme. Your choice is remembered on that computer.

Works with MasterScope

Liminal and MasterScope speak the same language. Learn's report opens in MasterScope with the result, a timeline of loudness and gain reduction, and your settings, and MasterScope's analysis tells you what to fix in the mix before Liminal ever has to work hard. The mastering workflow on the Learn page walks through it step by step.

Install on Windows

  1. Run the installer, or copy Liminal.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  2. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. In Ableton Live: Settings, Plug-Ins, Rescan.
  3. Put Liminal last on your master channel.

Install on macOS

  1. Open Liminal-1.1.0-macOS.pkg. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, click Open Anyway, and open it again. It installs the VST3 and the Audio Unit (untick either under Customise), and optionally MasterScope Meter (beta, macOS 14.2 or later).
  2. Open your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Logic checks Audio Units when it starts.
  3. Put Liminal last on your master channel.

What came in Liminal 1.0
  • Codec-safe ceiling, measured with real encoders. True peak, Streaming (Opus and Vorbis) or All codecs. Learn encodes your song, measures the decoded peaks and calibrates the limiter, so a loud master doesn't overshoot after upload.
  • A cleaner window: styles, a Tools panel for the helpers and an Advanced strip for set-and-forget settings.
  • Codec check: listen through Opus in real time, with the real decoded peak on the meter.
  • Learn by density. Aim for a PLR instead of a loudness, or match a reference track's density in one click.
  • Punch meter and Optimise. See how much the limiter takes off the hits, then let Optimise find the setup that keeps the most.
  • Punch Guard mode, a lookahead limiter into an oversampled clipper.
  • A detailed spectrum and a 2D and 3D spectrogram (input, output or delta, with the 16 kHz codec line and gain reduction on the same time line).
  • Mix Doctor, Measure a song, reference track and release pack inside the plug-in.
  • Stretch the window or go full screen. New instances remember the size.
  • Factory presets built around streaming-safe ceilings and density targets, and an About box with the licences.
  • A report with every Learn, and the Session log for any play-through. Open either straight in MasterScope (or save it and drop it on), which suggests what to change and gives you a preset to load back in.
