What's new in MasterScope and Liminal

Every release of MasterScope, Liminal and MasterScope Meter, newest first. Liminal checks nothing online, so this is where to see if there's a newer version.

1.0

1 November 2026

The first public release of all three tools. Free, no account, no ads in the apps.

MasterScope

  • A clear verdict: Release ready, Ready for streaming, Almost ready or Not ready yet, with the three numbers that matter and the next step.
  • Release ready: a platform-by-platform check for Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud and the club, with each issue tagged by the platforms it affects.
  • The full report: loudness over time, true peaks after streaming encoders, density, tone and spectrogram, stereo width and vectorscope, drum hits, where the peaks come from and what the limiter fights. Export it as a PDF or a share card.
  • Compare: set your track against a reference, or open two reports side by side.
  • Liminal suggestions: drop a Liminal report and MasterScope suggests setting changes, with the reason, as a preset to import.
  • Learn: a free guide to mastering for streaming, from loudness and peaks to tone, stereo and delivery.

Liminal, the mastering limiter

  • Windows and Mac: VST3 on Windows; VST3 and Audio Unit on Mac, native on Apple silicon and Intel.
  • Codec-safe ceiling: keeps the peaks listeners get after Opus (YouTube) and Ogg Vorbis (Spotify) under your ceiling, measured on your song with the real encoders.
  • Learn by loudness or density: aim for −14 LUFS, or for a PLR such as 10, and Learn sets the threshold in one listen.
  • Optimise: tries about 30 setups at your target, all equally loud, and ranks them by punch.
  • Punch Guard: a limiter into a clipper, so the drums keep their snap at the same loudness.
  • Mix Doctor: shows which part of the mix makes the peaks the limiter fights, and what to change.
  • Listen through Opus, a reference track at matched loudness, and a release pack that writes Streaming, Club, Apple Music and YouTube masters from one listen.
  • Spectrum, 2D and 3D spectrogram, punch meter, 14 factory presets, A/B, undo and session reports.

MasterScope Meter

  • Measure any song you can hear: a free Windows app that measures whatever Spotify, YouTube, VLC or your browser is playing.
  • A full report for any song: export a measurement and drop it on MasterScope for the same report you'd get from a file. Only numbers are kept, never the audio.
  • Use it as a reference: compare your mix with the songs you're up against.
